Independent Game Production

I think video games are the best way to relax and, just as importantly, a great way to connect with friends and share new experiences together. I believe no story or game mechanics can compare to the interactions between players. That's why I'm so passionate about multiplayer games.

If friends can jump into a game and have fun, it's a good game. If they can do this every day and feel something new and unique every time, it's a brilliant game.

That's, I believe, what i should aim for.
