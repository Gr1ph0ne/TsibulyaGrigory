About the Game

The year 2067. You are one of the few survivors of a nuclear war, hiding in a dark, creepy bunker riddled with mines. To survive, you’d better sweep all of them. But beware… some of them might be breathing.

That was our pitch at the project presentations after Xbox Game Camp Belgium 2026, a two-day game jam. The theme of the jam was “Minesweeper”. Our team of six managed to build a pretty solid game in that short time.

The goal is to clear the bunker of mines, some of which are mimics. Mimics are easy to spot, because they twitch quite a lot. But over time the bunker fills with toxic gas, which cuts visibility and makes the player’s job much harder. The player has to defuse the real mines and take out the mimics with a stun gun. Mix them up… and you’ll have to deal with the consequences. Defused mines can also be used to your advantage, to keep disturbed mimics at a distance.

This project was the first game I worked on as a team lead. It was a very important experience for me, because combining game design with team leadership is exactly what I’m aiming for.
