About the Game

Bloxiz is a game we made for a client project in the first semester of 2025.

You play as a farmer whose farm is surrounded by a cursed forest. Every day you find a couple of wounded creatures and bring them back to your farm to help them recover. In return for your care, the creatures help out around the farm until they are strong enough to go back to the wild.

When a creature leaves, it gives you a goodbye card. The goal is to collect every type of goodbye card.
